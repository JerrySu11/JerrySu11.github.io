Zihao (Jerry) Su

PhD candidate in Computer Science, UC Santa Barbara — SecLab, advised by Giovanni Vigna and Christopher Kruegel.

I study security, privacy, and user safety in emerging interactive systems, with a current focus on social virtual reality.

My work combines systems analysis, empirical measurement, and user studies to investigate information leakage, malicious user-generated content, and harassment.

I am interested in extending this work to the security and privacy challenges that arise when AI agents interact with people and other agents on users’ behalf.
