Zihao Su

PhD candidate in Computer Science, UC Santa Barbara — SecLab, advised by Giovanni Vigna and Christopher Kruegel.

Graduating June 2027 · seeking industry research scientist roles

I work on the security and privacy of systems that people share with strangers. Most of that work has been in social and multi-user virtual reality — showing that a headset can leak what you type, that a platform can quietly bend what you perceive, and that the harassment users report there can be measured and detected at scale.

The methods travel further than the medium. My work spans side-channel attacks, machine learning security, static program analysis, and large-scale user studies, at USENIX Security, NDSS, CHI, and NeurIPS.

Research

Attacks on social & multi-user VR

Shared virtual environments hand every participant a rich, continuous stream of other people's body motion. I have shown this stream is an exploitable side channel — recovering typed passwords and text from an avatar's movement in multi-user VR applications — and am now studying how the same openness enables harassment and malicious avatars at platform scale.

USENIX Security 2024 · two first-author manuscripts under review

Perception manipulation in VR/AR

A VR application controls what the user sees, so it can also control what they believe they did. I study attacks that redirect or misrepresent a user's own actions, and defenses that detect the manipulation from hand and body movement alone.

CHI 2025 (Honorable Mention) · CHI EA 2021

ML security & privacy compliance

Beyond VR: showing that invisible image watermarks are provably removable with generative diffusion models, and building cross-language code property graph analysis to check GDPR compliance in real WordPress plugins.

NeurIPS 2024 · NDSS 2023
