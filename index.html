Zihao Su

PhD candidate in Computer Science, UC Santa Barbara — SecLab, advised by Giovanni Vigna and Christopher Kruegel.

I work on the security and privacy of systems. Most of that work has been in Virtual Reality (e.g., showing that a headset can leak what you type, and that a platform can quietly bend what you perceive).

My work spans side-channel attacks, machine learning security, static program analysis, at USENIX Security, NDSS, CHI, and NeurIPS.
